# Code of Conduct

We are committed to making participation in this project a respectful,
constructive, and welcoming experience for everyone, regardless of background
or identity.

Examples of behavior that will not be accepted include harassment,
discrimination, personal attacks, sexualized language or attention, deliberate
intimidation, and publishing another person's private information without
their permission. Treat others with respect, give feedback about ideas and
work rather than people, and keep project discussions focused and professional.

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may limit or end
participation when behavior is harmful or disruptive.

To report a concern privately, contact the repository owner through
[their GitHub profile](https://github.com/passadis). Please do not report
conduct concerns in a public issue.
