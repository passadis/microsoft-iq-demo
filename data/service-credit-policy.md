# Service credit policy

Policy ID: CSP-014
Policy owner: Customer Operations Governance
Version: 4.2
Effective date: 2026-07-01
Classification: Internal
Approval status: Approved

## Purpose

This policy defines compensation for provider-caused missed on-site service appointments.

## Eligibility

A service credit may be offered when all of the following are true:

1. The appointment was confirmed in the scheduling system.
2. The customer was available during the confirmed service window.
3. The failure was caused by the provider and is recorded as `Provider no-show` or `Provider cancellation`.
4. The same incident has not already received compensation.

## Credit limits

- Standard credit: up to €75 for each eligible missed appointment.
- Multiple eligible appointments may be combined in one request.
- A frontline service agent may submit a combined request up to €150.
- Any request above €150 requires Customer Operations Manager approval before submission.
- Customer tier does not change these limits.

## Required evidence

The request must include the customer ID, appointment identifiers, incident dates, recorded provider-failure statuses, proposed amount, and a short policy-based reason.

## Prohibited behavior

- Do not promise or record a credit before the action system confirms submission.
- Do not split one request into multiple requests to avoid an approval threshold.
- Do not infer an exception from customer value, churn risk, or employee seniority.
