## What Contoso does

Contoso Home Services is a fictional global field-service company that helps residential and small-business customers maintain and repair their properties. Customers book electrical, plumbing, heating and cooling, appliance installation, and general handyman services through local service teams.

Contoso coordinates appointments with its own technicians and approved service partners. Each region manages local schedules and operating requirements, while the customer service team follows approved policies when resolving complaints and requesting service credits.

## How a service request moves through the business

1. A customer requests a service, such as an electrician visit or a plumbing repair.
2. Contoso creates a service case and schedules an appointment with a technician or service partner.
3. Appointment updates record events such as confirmation, technician dispatch, arrival, completion, cancellation, or a missed visit.
4. If something goes wrong, Customer Operations reviews the case and the appointment history against the current policy and runbook.
5. An authorized operator reviews any proposed service-credit request before it is submitted.
